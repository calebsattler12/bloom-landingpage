## Hard rules for all Bloom marketing copy

1. **Zero em dashes.** Use commas, periods, colons, or parentheses.
2. **No negative parallelism.** Do not write "It's not X, it's Y," "This isn't
   about… It's about…," "not just X, but Y," or "more than just."
3. **Banned diction** (do not use): delve, leverage (verb), seamless, robust,
   crucial, vital, foster, unlock, unleash, pivotal, cutting-edge,
   groundbreaking, revolutionize, transformative, game-changer, holistic,
   synergy, journey/landscape/navigate (metaphorical filler), empower,
   streamline, resonate, tapestry, myriad, plethora.
4. **No formulaic openers** ("In today's fast-paced…", "With the rise of AI…").
5. **No compulsive summarizing** ("Overall," "In conclusion," "Ultimately" as
   a recap closer).
6. **Specificity over puffery.** If a sentence could appear on another
   company's site unchanged, rewrite it.
7. **Proof ethics:** no fabricated testimonials, hours-saved stats, or
   unverified partner claims. State goals as goals, results only when
   documented.
8. **LinkedIn:** paste-only. No emoji unless asked. One idea per post. Soft
   Book a Call CTA is fine.

## Tone for Bloom

Direct, concrete, warm enough for B2B founders. Short sentences. Lead with
the point. End on the result or the next step, not hype.

## Pricing in public drafts

Do **not** publish dollar amounts, retainers, or install fees in
`index.html`, `pitch-deck.html`, or `linkedin-content.md`. Qualitative cost
language only (own the hardware, month to month, fraction of a content team)
if needed. Prefer Book a Call over a price list.
