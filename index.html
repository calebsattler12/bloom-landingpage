The problem

Most B2B content is a guess.

It gets written by someone imagining what buyers care about, then tuned to a keyword tool. So it reads like everyone else's, ranks for nothing that converts, and never gets cited when a buyer asks an AI assistant for a recommendation.

No one to write it

A one-to-five person marketing team is already running campaigns, events and the site. Long-form content is the first thing to slip and the last thing to get picked back up.

Generic AI output

Pointing a chatbot at a keyword produces filler — nothing grounded in your buyers, your deals, or your actual point of view. Readers can tell, and so can search.

The evidence goes unused

You have hundreds of recorded calls in Granola, Circleback, Fathom or Gong — the clearest record anywhere of what buyers ask and worry about. Almost nobody mines it for content.

How it works

Five steps. The first two happen before you sign anything.

We start by analysing your calls and handing you a findings report. If you stop there, you keep it — it's built to stand on its own.

Step 5 loops back into step 4 — performance decides what gets written next.

Why it's different

Evidence, not assumptions.

01

Two-pass analysis

We analyse your calls first, on their own, and derive themes from the raw data. Only then do we load your stated ICP and positioning — and report the delta. Feeding both in together would just bias the model toward confirming what you already believe. So we don't.

02

Published under a real name

Never a faceless company account. Every piece goes out under your CEO or a genuine in-house expert, because attributed content performs materially better and reads as human. LinkedIn posts are always copy-paste by that person — we never post to anyone's personal account.

03

A human gate on everything

Drafts clear a shared QA and brand-voice check, then go to you in Slack or Teams. Approval schedules a piece — it never publishes it instantly. Revisions are capped at two rounds before a human is pulled in, so nothing loops forever unattended.

04

Judgment where it counts

Mechanical work — fetching, scanning, scheduling — runs on dedicated hardware we install in your office. Anything you'll actually read, or that reasons from evidence to a conclusion, runs on frontier models. That split is a deliberate quality and cost decision, not a slogan.
