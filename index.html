The gap

AI names someone else when your site does not answer the question.

    Buyers ask AI a specific question. You have no page built for it.

  • No industry or comparison pages

    Shortlist and “vs” questions go to whoever wrote them first.

    AI cites pages Google can find. Thin sites stay invisible.

    robots.txt can quietly block the bots that read your site.

FAQ

Questions founders ask

What is the free snapshot, and what do you need from me to make it?

The free snapshot shows how often AI names you for buyer questions in your category, and which sources it cites. We need your website URL and a work email. Optional competitors improve the comparison.

Do I need to send call recordings to get the snapshot?

No. The snapshot is built from your website and category questions. Call data is used later, after we start working together.

How do pages get published?

Your team approves every draft in Slack. Then it goes to Webflow or WordPress. Nothing ships to the live site without a human yes.

Who is this for?

US founders and CEOs of B2B software, SaaS, or professional services companies, about 10 to 25 employees, with a salesperson but weak inbound.

How long until the first draft?

After onboarding access on day 0, foundation work lands around day 7, the first calibration draft around day 9, and weekly reporting at day 14.

How does pricing work?

Three publishing levels. Price is set on a call. Starting at TODO_STARTING_PRICE.

Will you post to our LinkedIn or site without approval?

No. Site drafts need a human yes in Slack before publish. LinkedIn is paste-only and never auto-posted.

Email caleb@runbloom.ai, or book a call.
