  • Re-derived from the full call vault each month, then compared with last month.
  • Buyer language the rest of the team has to use.

The monthly report

ICP findings report. Delivered week 1. Eleven public sections. The evidence appendix stays internal.

  1. Executive synthesis: three patterns, the clearest buying trigger, the largest message gap
  2. Evidence base and method: what was included, what was excluded, and why
  3. Best-client roster and commercial LTV: ranked by lifetime, retention, and call evidence, not case-study polish
  4. Derived ICP snapshot: segment, trigger, primary pain, why they act, confidence
  5. Buying committee: persona, job to be done, objection, message that lands
  6. What buyers actually say: verbatim, what it signals, the underlying problem
  7. Stated ICP vs derived ICP: the delta table
  8. Qualification boundaries: best fit, possible fit, disqualifier
  9. Month-over-month drift: new themes, fading themes, new objections, segment movement
  10. Implications for content and positioning: lead with, prove with, create next, de-emphasize, preserve
  11. Decisions and next actions: action, owner, due, status
