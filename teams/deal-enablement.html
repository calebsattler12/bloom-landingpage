Deal Enablement Director

Win-loss from the calls, not only from the CRM field. Battlecards and coaching from the same vault.

On the always-on team

You own the Mac mini. Bloom installs it in your office. Day to day you talk to the Orchestrator in Slack. A human from Bloom stays. This seat is one employee on that roster, not a project that ends at kickoff.

What this seat does

Owns why deals are actually won and lost, from the calls rather than the loss-reason field. Same vault as Customer Intelligence, different lens.

  • Reconciles what the CRM says with what was said on the calls.
  • Sample size stated on every rate. Directional when n is small.
  • Coaching and battlecards the floor can use, between reports, in Slack.

The monthly report

Win-loss report. Delivered day 1. Eleven public sections. The evidence appendix stays internal.

  1. Executive synthesis: the dominant win, the dominant loss, and what changed
  2. Win rate by segment, using the same slices as the ICP pack, with sample size on every row
  3. Why deals were won, with the language from the calls
  4. Why deals were lost: loss taxonomy, ranked
  5. Competitor involvement: who showed up, in which deals, and what was said
  6. Objection ranking, and which objections travel with loss
  7. Stage drop: where deals stall, and what happened on the call before they did
  8. CRM reconciliation: where the recorded reason disagrees with the calls
  9. Expansion and referral signals
  10. Recommended messaging fixes for the rest of the team
  11. Decisions and next actions
