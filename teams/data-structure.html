On the always-on team

You own the Mac mini. Bloom installs it in your office. Day to day you talk to the Orchestrator in Slack. A human from Bloom stays. This seat is one employee on that roster, not a project that ends at kickoff.

What this seat does

The quiet seat. The other employees depend on it. Working memory stays in order so the rest of the seats are not guessing from a messy pile.

  • Keeps sources and working files where the team can find them.
  • Flags when the pile is getting messy, or when a connected tool stops talking.
  • Stays in the background on purpose.

The monthly check

Delivered day 1. Mostly for the team. The client-facing version is a short proof that the seats are working from sources that still stand up, not from a pile nobody trusted.

  1. Whether the working files are in order, and what is getting stale
  2. Topics the seats keep hitting with nothing solid behind them
  3. Who still needs to sit down so a gap can be filled
  4. Whether connected tools actually synced, and when they last did
  5. Recommended fixes, with owner and due date
