Background

Bloom is a done-for-you service that builds and maintains a content library for B2B companies. The public entry point is a free snapshot of how a company shows up for buyer questions. The Bloom team turns call data and expert interviews into pages a buyer can cite. Clients approve every draft in Slack before Bloom publishes to Webflow or WordPress.

Buyers and AI assistants can cite those pages. Bloom handles technical health, visibility reporting, and interview-backed content. Client time is mostly a short interview and Slack approvals.

Core offerings

  • Free snapshot: Website URL + work email (optional competitors). Shows how often AI names the company for buyer questions and which sources it cites. Call recordings are not required for the snapshot.
  • Sample snapshot: Blurred example at /snapshot/sample/
  • Free 15-minute Loom teardown: Quieter fallback offer when a visitor wants a short recorded review instead of the snapshot form
  • Technical health: Site and crawl readiness so AI systems and search engines can find and use the pages
  • Visibility reporting: Bloom tracks how often the company appears in AI answers and related search signals, then sends weekly email reports on paid work
  • Interview-backed content: Voice-agent interviews of the company’s people, then Bloom writes answer-first pages in the expert’s voice
  • Slack approval: Nothing ships to the live site without a human yes. LinkedIn is paste-only and never auto-posted
  • Publishing: Bloom publishes to Webflow or WordPress after Slack approval
  • Content pipeline: Inputs (call data, brand voice, GTM). Bloom builds the knowledge layer (ICP, buyer questions, knowledge map, glossary, interviews). Bloom crafts voice and style. Bloom rewrites pages for AI and humans (tables, FAQ blocks, schema). Client reviews in Slack. Bloom publishes
  • On-site SEO and AEO: Buyer-question pages, industry and comparison coverage, indexing and crawl fixes
  • Off-site work (Scale tier): Backlinks and directories, Reddit management, LinkedIn content management, and similar channels

Ideal for

  • US founders and CEOs of B2B software, SaaS, or professional services companies
  • Teams of about 10 to 25 people
  • Companies that have a salesperson but weak inbound
  • Teams that want to show up when buyers ask AI for the best tool in their category
  • Leaders who want citeable pages grounded in real expert interviews, not generic AI-only drafts

How it works

  1. Start with the free snapshot (URL + email; optional competitors)
  2. Client shares inputs: call data, brand voice, and GTM info
  3. Bloom builds the knowledge layer (ICP analysis, buyer questions, knowledge map, glossary, interviews)
  4. Bloom crafts voice and style from expert interviews, tone, positioning, and brand voice
  5. Bloom rewrites pages with structure both AI and humans can read (tables, FAQ blocks, schema)
  6. Client approves in Slack; Bloom revises, then publishes to Webflow or WordPress
  7. Bloom measures with weekly email reporting and ongoing visibility tracking (cadence depends on retainer tier)

Done for you, from inputs to publishing, taking inbound leads off of your plate. Typical early timeline after onboarding access on day 0: foundation work around day 7, first calibration draft around day 9, weekly reporting at day 14.
