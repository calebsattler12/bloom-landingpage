Background

Bloom helps B2B companies get named in AI answers and Google search. The public entry point is a free snapshot of how a company shows up for buyer questions. If the gap is real, Bloom turns call data and expert interviews into citeable pages. Humans approve every draft in Slack before anything publishes to Webflow or WordPress.

Positioning thesis: get named in AI search. Call data becomes citeable pages through technical health, visibility reporting, and interview-backed content with Slack approval.

Core offerings

  • Free snapshot: Website URL + work email (optional competitors). Shows how often AI names the company for buyer questions and which sources it cites. Call recordings are not required for the snapshot.
  • Sample snapshot: Blurred example at /snapshot/sample/
  • Free 15-minute Loom teardown: Quieter fallback offer when a visitor wants a short recorded review instead of the snapshot form
  • Technical health: Site and crawl readiness so AI systems and search engines can find and use the pages
  • Visibility reporting: How often the company appears in AI answers and related search signals, with a live AI visibility dashboard on paid work
  • Interview-backed content: Voice-agent interviews of the company’s people, then answer-first pages in the expert’s voice
  • Slack approval: Nothing ships to the live site without a human yes. LinkedIn is paste-only and never auto-posted
  • Publishing: Webflow or WordPress
  • Content pipeline: Inputs (call data, brand voice, GTM) → foundation (ICP, buyer questions, knowledge map, glossary) → interview briefs → expert interviews → content → review loop → client publishes
  • On-site SEO and AEO: Buyer-question pages, industry and comparison coverage, indexing and crawl fixes
  • Off-site work (Scale tier): Backlinks and directories, Reddit management, LinkedIn content management, and similar channels

Ideal for

  • US founders and CEOs of B2B software, SaaS, or professional services companies
  • Teams of about 10 to 25 people
  • Companies that have a salesperson but weak inbound
  • Teams that want to show up when buyers ask AI for the best tool in their category
  • Leaders who want citeable pages grounded in real expert interviews, not generic AI-only drafts

How it works

  1. Start with the free snapshot (URL + email; optional competitors)
  2. If the gap is real, plan from ICP, Google Search Console, GA4, and buyer questions
  3. Run voice-agent interviews of the company’s people
  4. Draft answer-first pages; humans approve every draft in Slack
  5. Publish to Webflow or WordPress
  6. Measure with weekly email reporting and a live AI visibility dashboard (cadence depends on retainer tier)

Typical early timeline after onboarding access on day 0: foundation work around day 7, first calibration draft around day 9, weekly reporting at day 14.

Pricing

  • Scale: Everything in Growth, plus off-site SEO (backlinks, directories, Reddit, LinkedIn content management, similar channels), weekly reports
