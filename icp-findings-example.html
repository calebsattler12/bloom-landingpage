Three patterns, one trigger

Section 1 of 11

Executive synthesis

Across about 3,347 Circleback meetings and the commercial LTV register, three patterns defined the strongest opportunities: the inherited, untrusted CRM; post-migration orphan attribution; and headcount before systems. The clearest buying trigger was a new GTM leader in the first 90 days in seat. The largest gap between the market and the current message: buyers say “prove ROI to the CEO” and the site says “attribution modeling.”

#PatternWhat it isConfidence
P1 Inherited, untrusted CRM A new leader inherits a CRM they cannot trust (“mess / black box”) and has 90 days to show progress. High
P2 Post-migration orphan attribution CRM was migrated or “implemented,” but reporting, lifecycle, and attribution were never rebuilt. Marketing looks like it drives nothing (“Direct bucket”). High
P3 Headcount before systems The board wants more sellers. The champion knows the funnel is broken, and hiring first “burns through reps.” Medium
“Marketing can’t prove ROI to the CEO.”

Two-pass method

Section 2 of 11

Evidence base and method

Bloom used a two-pass analysis so a positioning slide could not set the ICP.

  • Pass one: calls only. Themes, pain, triggers, objections, and buyer language were derived before internal positioning documents were visible.
  • Pass two: comparison. The derived profile was compared with the client’s stated ICP to find alignment, blind spots, and disagreement.
  • Qualification filter. Only calls matched to the approved best-client list (Section 3) and qualification rules (Section 8) were included.

What content should do

Section 10 of 11

Implications for content and positioning

  • Lead with: “Prove ROI to the CEO,” “Direct bucket,” “Don’t hire another seller until the funnel exists,” post-migration orphan attribution. Speak to R1 and R2 on SEO and money pages.
  • Prove with: Commercial LTV accounts from Section 3 where public-safe, plus keep stories that survive copy hygiene. Every headline number must also appear in the body. Never invent metrics.
  • Create next: Choose-agency vetting (“Did they design sync and attribution, or just connect the pipes?”). HubSpot ↔ Salesforce sync diagnostic. A retainer-scale case study when consent allows.
  • De-emphasize: Generic “RevOps education.” “Attribution modeling” / “multi-touch” as headlines. PE via keyword stuffing. Any excluded logo as proof.
  • Preserve: Keep stories that still match the vault. The public PE → exit narrative on LinkedIn.
