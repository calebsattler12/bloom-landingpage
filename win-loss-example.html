Executive synthesis

This period the client closed 3 of 13 labeled deals (about 23% win rate, directional). The dominant win: the buyer already knew their CRM or attribution was lying, and a signer was willing to pay for an ongoing partner, not tickets. The dominant loss: the day-to-day contact liked the work, but the budget or board would not put RevOps ahead of hiring sellers (or cutting spend).

Versus the Sep 24 baseline: September late-stage is board-gate heavy. Only one September closed-won. The closed-lost set expanded. A Contract Sent cluster is still open as of the Sep 25 pull, and is not counted as wins or losses yet.
