Section 1 of 11

Executive synthesis

This period, the client closed 4 of 9 labeled deals (about 44% win rate, directional, n = 9). The dominant reason deals closed: the buyer already knew their CRM or attribution was lying, and a signer was willing to pay for an ongoing partner, not tickets. The dominant reason they did not: the day-to-day contact liked the work, but the budget or board would not put RevOps ahead of hiring sellers (or cutting spend). This is the first live build, so there is no month-over-month change yet.
