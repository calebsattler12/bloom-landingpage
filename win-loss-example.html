Why deals actually closed

Won and retained patterns

Accounts that stayed shared a forcing function. The buyer hired a third party who made the black box speak, then handed the champion a page the CEO could read.

  • Inherited CRM, fast trust (R1). Maps to Noonlight and Whistic. New in-seat leaders closed when RevBlack showed up as the person who bangs the drum. Continuity when the sole RevOps expert left beat another documentation project. Noonlight public line that still lands: “By the end of April, we had closed more net new deals in 2025 than we did in all of 2024.” Do not lean on Whistic’s unsupported “3×” until it is sourced.
  • Visibility before more campaigns (R2). Maps to Property Vista (CTR 1.5% to 5.05%, same team and budget), GRIN, Cariloop, Reveal. Operators closed when the offer named the Direct bucket and forensic reporting. “You don’t have a content problem. You have a visibility problem” is the re-frame. Cariloop: “We actually believe and understand what’s in our pipeline now.”
  • Stop waiting on the backlog (R3). Revenue execs closed when pipeline reviews stopped using 30-day-old data and the spreadsheet stopped being the real CRM. Call volume is high. Public Top-10 proof is still thin (OpenBrand partial, Biscom adjacent). First retainer-scale blind-spot path in the vault: ROI Rocket, about 80 hours / 6 months (Aug 24). Keep that named only where consent allows.
  • PE arc and commercial gravity (R4 + LTV pole). Biscom is the PE to exit narrative. Lazer Logistics is the commercial pole: about $30K/mo, 2+ years, estimated lifetime about $660K to $720K+. Northwoods renewal signed (80 hrs / 6 mo, about $200K 12-month view). Kalibrate upgrade path (about 80 to 180 hrs). Salient: demand above 40 hrs/mo, attribution plus spreadsheet-forecast pain.
  • Ammo for upstairs. Wins included an executive summary the champion could forward. Feature menus did not survive that handoff. GTM still cites Revenue Exec at 89% DM win rate. That persona is under-proved on the public site.

Where champions stall upstairs

Lost, stalled, and do-not-model

Losses clustered on clarity and sequencing, not on whether the champion liked the team. Theme E in the vault is the deal-cycle pattern: the champion is bought, the CEO/CFO/board was not in the room, the deal dies politely.

  • Unclear get. “Didn’t come away with a clear understanding of what [RevBlack] is or what they’d be getting.” The champion cannot repeat the offer in a hallway.
  • Cheaper alternative. “He left skeptical enough to say he’d call cheaper, more experienced alternatives.”
  • No push to the decision maker. “He remains open… but won’t push it to the decision maker without stronger justification.” “We’re back to a decision point in one to two weeks.”
  • Headcount before systems (R5). Aliri and Teletrac board/L1 gates. “Adding headcount without fixing top-of-funnel infrastructure will repeat burning through reps.” “Board approval is the blocker, not internal alignment.”
  • Operator without a sponsor (R2). Highest-research persona (organic search about 50% of this persona’s deals, plus Coastal Consulting referrals). Highest average deal size in the journey deck: $11,208. Stalls as a standalone contact. Ask “Who signs off above you?” inside the first two conversations. New in-seat GTM leaders sometimes hold budget ($10K to $20K retainer) and still need Revenue Executive sign-off.

What Sales Enablement works first

Implications for RevBlack sales and site

Sales Enablement inherits this vault the same way Customer Intelligence does. Call query in Slack is the daily surface. Win-loss, battlecards, and coaching are the artifacts.

  1. Stop using TalkDesk as proof. Ops can stay active. Ideal and the homepage cannot.
  2. Coach to buyer words: inherited mess, Direct bucket, shadow spreadsheet, prove ROI to the CEO, 26,000 contacts with no reason recorded. Drop “attribution modeling” on a first call.
  3. Prefer commercial LTV logos when citing proof (Lazer, Northwoods, Kalibrate, Salient, Property Vista, Biscom), after a public-safe check.
  4. Build the upstairs one-pager as a standard output. Theme E losses die in that gap. Productize “don’t hire another seller until the funnel exists.”
  5. Qualify the operator path with a named exec sponsor in the first two conversations. $11K average deal size still stalls without a signer.
  6. Keep revenue-exec proof in referral packs. They will not find you on a keyword. Close the Seg 2 case-study gap when ROI Rocket (or equivalent) is consent-safe.
  7. New in-seat GTM leaders (including Head of Sales) are the hottest live champions and the thinnest site shelf. Inheritance language belongs on the inherited-CRM money page.
