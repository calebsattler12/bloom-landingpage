Two or three moves this month

Section 1 of 9

Executive summary

Three moves are worth acting on this month. Together they stay inside a lean six-month ceiling and put the client in rooms where tracked competitors are not.

  • Apply to the owner-operator retreat (Nashville, Mar 15 to 17). Applications close Oct 21. It is capped at a small cohort of founders and executives from brands and operators. Public marketplace data on that community matches the client's buyers. We found none of the tracked competitors connected to it. Ask about partner membership too, because it can waive the listed seat fee (figures masked).
  • Treat the large Las Vegas logistics show as a meetings trip. Do not buy a booth. Two Gold-sponsor competitors and several other tracked vendors are already on the floor. Bring two people on discounted early-stage passes, book meetings with operator attendees before the show, and host a small dinner for owners.
  • Pitch a practitioner association session with a customer leading it. The call for sessions closes Oct 2. The association only accepts vendor proposals when a practitioner is the lead presenter. A live-customer billing or quoting result fits that rule. The deadline is tight, so this only works if the customer says yes in time.

Also recommended: walk the floor at the marketplace-seller show in Las Vegas on Mar 2 to 3. Many operators exhibit there to win marketplace sellers, so the owners are standing at their booths. Pitch four podcasts, starting with the highest-fit operator show. Start partner conversations with the matching marketplace and an alt-carrier network. Skip the warehouse-association booth, the enterprise supply-chain conference, the retail/brand show, and a materials-handling booth this cycle. Reasons sit in Section 2.

Post-event debrief

Section 8 of 9

Post-event debriefs

Small-parcel forum, Sep 14 to 16, 2026. Thousands of attendees and a large exhibitor floor. The client's public recap says the team was there throughout.

  • What competitors did (public): Competitor C ran its own customer conference at the same resort the day before, then took a booth and a session. Competitor E's CEO spoke. Competitors B, D, and H were listed as present.
  • What showed up on stage: Carrier diversification, cost intelligence over cost cutting, and demand for unified billing and tracking. All three support the client's positioning.
  • What we still need from the client to finish this debrief: number of conversations, qualified operator leads, meetings booked since, total cost, and which competitor booths drew the most traffic. Those let us calculate cost per qualified lead and set the next-cycle budget.
